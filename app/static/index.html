Import from LinkedIn

  1. On LinkedIn go to Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data (the page is titled “Download my data”).
  2. Choose the first option, Download larger data archive. It is the one that includes connections. If your page offers a Connections checkbox under “Want something in particular?”, ticking only that also works and is quicker.
  3. Click Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a download link, which can take up to 24 hours for the larger archive and stays valid for 72 hours.
  4. Unzip the download and upload the file named Connections.csv here.

Re-import every few weeks to pick up new connections. Each import replaces the previous list.

How a company is listed

LinkedIn's file gives one employer per connection: the company on their current position, written the way they wrote it. Someone who interned at a company last summer, or starts there next year, is listed under whatever they have now (often their university), so they won't count. Search here to see what the app has to work with. It shows employer names and how many connections, never who.
