Import from LinkedIn

  1. On LinkedIn go to Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose "Want something in particular?", tick Connections, and click Request archive.
  3. LinkedIn emails you a download link (usually within about 10 minutes). Unzip it and upload Connections.csv here.

Re-import every few weeks to pick up new connections. Each import replaces the previous list.
